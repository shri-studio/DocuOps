Frequently asked questions

Is it really free? ⌄
Yes. The Free plan includes our two core tools with no credit card required. You can process up to 50 documents per month completely free.
Do I need to install anything? ⌄
No. DocuOps runs entirely in your Chrome or Edge browser. No download, no setup, no IT department needed.
Does my data get uploaded anywhere? ⌄
Never. All document processing happens in your browser. Your files never leave your device. Not even to our servers.
Does it work with Arabic documents? ⌄
Yes. DocuOps supports Arabic, English and mixed-language documents. Full RTL support included.
Can my whole team use it? ⌄
Yes. The Team plan supports up to 10 users with shared templates and learning profiles.
Does it work offline? ⌄
Tools 1 and 2 work fully offline after first load. AI-powered tools require an internet connection.
How accurate is the OCR? ⌄
Our OCR engine (Tesseract.js) achieves 95%+ accuracy on clear documents. The select-area mode improves accuracy further for specific fields.
Can I use my own Excel template? ⌄
Yes. Upload your existing Excel file and DocuOps automatically converts your columns into form fields.
